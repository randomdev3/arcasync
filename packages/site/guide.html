From install to a shared team folder

Install the plugin, sign in, make a team, share a folder, invite people. About five minutes.

01Before you start

  • Obsidian 1.11.4 or newer, on desktop or mobile.
  • A Google or GitHub account. No account there? A teammate can send you an invite link or PIN instead, and you pick a name and a password.

02Install the plugin

With BRAT recommended

ArcaSync isn't in the community plugin list. BRAT installs it from GitHub and keeps it updated.

  1. Open Obsidian Settings → Community plugins. If Restricted mode is on, turn it off.
  2. Click Browse, search for “BRAT” (by TfTHacker), then Install and Enable.
  3. Open the command palette and run BRAT: Plugins: Add a beta plugin for testing (with or without version).
  4. Paste the repository and confirm:
    {{repo}}
  5. Back in Community plugins, enable ArcaSync.
  6. Updates: BRAT checks when Obsidian starts. To check now, run BRAT: Plugins: Check for updates to all beta plugins and UPDATE.

Or install by hand

  1. Download main.js, manifest.json and styles.css from the latest release at .
  2. Create the folder <your vault>/.obsidian/plugins/arcasync/ and put the three files in it. .obsidian is hidden: on macOS press Cmd+Shift+. in Finder; on Windows use View → Show → Hidden items.
  3. In Settings → Community plugins, click reload (↻), then enable ArcaSync.

03Sign in

  1. Open Settings → ArcaSync.
  2. Click Continue with Google or Continue with GitHub. Your browser opens; sign in there.
  3. The browser shows which device is asking, for example Obsidian on MacBook. Check it and click Approve. Obsidian finishes signing in.

Other ways in:

  • Sign in with name & password, if you joined with an invite and set a password.
  • Have an invite link or PIN? Paste it in the plugin, or open the link and join in the browser.

Already signed in on another device? Start the sign-in on the new one and approve its code on the approve page.

04Create a team

In Settings → ArcaSync → Teams, click Create team and name it. You become its owner. You can also do it on your account page.

Joining someone else's team instead? Skip to invites.

05Share a folder

  1. Open your team in Settings → ArcaSync.
  2. Click Share a local folder and pick a folder from your vault. Or right-click a folder in the file explorer and choose Share with team….
  3. Teammates see it under the team and click Add to this vault to choose where it lives on their side.

Folders can't be inside each other. Notes already in the folder upload. If a teammate has a note at the same path, the texts are merged; when they can't be merged cleanly, your version is kept as a local copy next to it, so nothing is lost.

06Invite teammates

Owners invite from the team page in Obsidian or on your account page:

  • Invite link: send it privately. It works once and expires after 7 days. Your teammate opens it, signs in with Google or GitHub (or picks a name), and is in.
  • PIN: a 6-digit code to read out or type. It works once and lasts 72 hours. They enter it at /join or under Have an invite link or PIN? in the plugin.

Pick a role for each: owners manage the team, editors write, viewers read. Joining a team gives access to all its folders.

07Working together

  • Coloured carets with names are live, and so are selections.
  • Avatars of who's here appear in the status bar and on the folder.
  • ArcaSync: Jump to user… opens their note at their cursor. ArcaSync: Follow user… keeps following them until you move.
  • Attachments (images, PDFs, anything) sync too, up to 200 MB each.
  • Viewers can read but not edit.

08Self-hosting

The plugin connects to the ArcaSync server out of the box. Running your own? Set it in Settings → ArcaSync → Self-hosting → Server URL. The default is:

{{relay}}

Everyone on a team uses the same server. An invite opened with Open in Obsidian fills in its server after you confirm.

09Troubleshooting

The browser didn't come back to Obsidian

Finish in the browser, approve the device, then switch back to Obsidian. It signs in within a few seconds. If it gave up, click the sign-in button again.

“This link is not a valid ArcaSync invite”

The link was cut off. Copy all of it, including everything after invite=.

The invite page says “already used”

If that was you, sign in. Otherwise ask for a new invite.

The code expired

Codes last 10 minutes. Start the sign-in again.

“Session expired — connect again to sign in”

Sessions last 90 days. Sign in again.

Nothing syncs

Is the folder added to this vault under your team, and does the status show it connected? Disconnect and connect it again from the folder's menu.

On mobile

Install BRAT the same way, then sign in with Google or GitHub, or approve a code from your desktop.

Updating

BRAT updates on startup. For a manual install, replace the three files.

Leaving a team

Click Leave team on the team page or on your account page. The files in your folder stay.
