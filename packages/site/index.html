Obsidian plugin / invite-only

one folder
every cursor

ArcaSync keeps a folder of your vault live between friends. See each other type, jump to anyone's cursor, follow along. Attachments come too.

Install guide

01 / Features

What it does

Live carets

Every collaborator's cursor and selection, in their colour, as they type.

Presence, jump, follow

See who's in the folder. Jump to their cursor, or follow them around.

Files, not just notes

Images, PDFs and other attachments sync too, up to 200 MB each.

Invites, not sign-ups

Accounts only come from an invite. Sign-ins are approved with a code.
